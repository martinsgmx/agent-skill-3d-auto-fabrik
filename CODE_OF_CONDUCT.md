# Code of conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html),
abbreviated here for convenience — the linked text governs in case of
conflict.

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this community a harassment-free
experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity,
sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- being kind and constructive, especially when reviewing safety-critical
  wording,
- accepting responsibility for mistakes and learning from them,
- focusing on what is best for the community and for people operating
  printers.

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- trolling, insulting/derogatory comments, personal or political attacks,
- publishing others' private information (doxing),
- deliberate weakening of safety gates for a demo, joke, or benchmark
  screenshot.

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported to the maintainers via
the repository's private contact channel (see `SECURITY.md` for reporting
sensitive matters; ordinary conduct concerns use the same route with a
non-security subject). Maintainers will acknowledge, investigate, and act,
up to and including temporary or permanent bans.
